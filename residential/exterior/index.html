Exteriors Built for Colorado Weather.

Front Range siding takes hail in June, 100-degree sun in July, and a freeze-thaw cycle that pries paint off wood all winter. A repaint that lasts here is a prep job first and a paint job second. That's why we back ours for 10 years.

The Difference Is Underneath

What a 10-Year Exterior Takes

Power wash. Scrape and sand every failing edge. Prime bare wood instead of waving at it. Re-caulk gaps and joints so water stays out. Then, and only then, Behr Ultra Exterior paint and primer in two coats. Skipping any of those steps makes a bid cheaper and a repaint sooner. We don't skip them, and we put a 10-year workmanship warranty in writing to prove it.

One honest note: for exteriors we strongly prefer walking the property before locking a firm price. Sun-baked south walls and hidden wood rot are real, and finding them early keeps your quote true.

  • Wood, stucco, and fiber cement siding
  • Fascia, soffits, trim, shutters, and front doors
  • Fences, decks, and railings on request
  • HOA color scheme matching and approvals paperwork
  • Two-coat application, premium exterior topcoats
