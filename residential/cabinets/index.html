A New Kitchen for the Price of a Paint Job.

Replacing cabinets runs tens of thousands and takes your kitchen offline for weeks. Refinishing the boxes and doors you already own gets you a sprayed, factory-smooth finish in days, at a fraction of the cost.

Why Sprayed, Not Brushed

The Finish Is the Whole Point

Brushed cabinet paint shows every stroke and chips at the corners within a year. Our process is different: degrease, sand, bonding primer, then sprayed enamel coats that cure hard and wipe clean. Doors and drawer fronts get sprayed flat in a controlled setup, boxes get masked and finished in place.

Color change, hardware swap, and small repairs all ride in the same scope. And like every interior job we do, the work carries a 5-year workmanship warranty.

  • Kitchen and bathroom cabinets, built-ins, and vanities
  • Sprayed factory-smooth enamel finish
  • Full color changes, including dark-to-light
  • New hardware installation on request
  • Kitchen back in service in days, not weeks
  • 5-year workmanship warranty
