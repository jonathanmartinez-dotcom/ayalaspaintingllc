Our Paint Philosophy

Why We Paint With Behr

Most painters spec whatever earns them the biggest supplier kickback. We paint with Behr Ultra because after fourteen years on Colorado walls and siding, we believe it's the better paint. Not the cheaper option. The better paint.

Our contractor pricing on it gets passed through to your quote, which is a real number you can compare. Want Sherwin-Williams instead? We'll price it for you honestly, no upcharge games either way.

And the quote itself works like a receipt: line items that visibly add up to the total. No mystery gap between the subtotal and what you pay.

  • Free flat-rate estimate, usually within 24 hours
  • Contractor paint discounts passed through to your price
  • The same named crew from first day to final walkthrough
  • Job site left clean at the end of every day, start to finish
  • Drywall repair and texture-match included in the paint scope
  • Warranty in writing: 10-year exterior, 5-year interior
