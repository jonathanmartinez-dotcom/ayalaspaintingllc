HOA Repaints Without the Board Meeting Circus.

A community repaint has three audiences: the board that votes, the management company that coordinates, and the homeowners who live with the result. We write scopes for the first, run schedules for the second, and leave clean job sites for the third.

Built for Reserve Budgets

Phased Cycles, Predictable Numbers

Most communities can't repaint everything in one season, and reserve studies don't like surprises. So we bid in phases: this year's buildings, next year's buildings, firm per-phase pricing the board can plan around. Color standards get documented once and matched exactly across every phase, even years apart.

Homeowner communication is on us, not your property manager: posted notices before crews arrive, driveways and landscaping protected, and a direct line to our on-site lead for the resident who has questions. COI, W-9, and references come with the bid.

  • Building exteriors on phased, multi-year repaint cycles
  • Clubhouses, pools, fitness rooms, and common interiors
  • Perimeter fences, monuments, and entry features
  • Metal railing and stair rehab, including rust treatment
  • Documented color standards for consistent phases
  • 5-year commercial workmanship warranty
