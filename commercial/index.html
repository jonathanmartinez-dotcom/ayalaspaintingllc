How We Operate

Built for the Way Your Office Works

You manage properties or a facility. You don't have time to chase a painter for an invoice, a COI, or a straight answer on when building C gets done. So we removed those problems.

Every commercial engagement gets a named on-site project manager you can call directly, plus owner-level quality checks that never show up on your bill. Invoices go out weekly with labor broken out by task and materials passed through at cost, receipts attached. If your accounting team wants ACH, we're set up for it.

Bid structure matches the job. Defined scopes get fixed pricing. Rolling maintenance and rehab work runs on documented T&M under a master service agreement, so you can start work without re-bidding every task.

  • COI and W-9 delivered before the first crew day, usually within 24 hours of request
  • Fixed-bid or T&M under MSA, structured to how your budget works
  • Weekly invoicing with labor detail and materials at cost, receipts attached
  • Scheduling around tenants, services, and school calendars, including nights and weekends
  • 5-year commercial workmanship warranty in writing
  • Licensed and insured, Est. 2012, Thornton CO
