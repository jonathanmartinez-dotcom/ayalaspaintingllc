Fourteen Years In, Still Answering Our Own Phone.

Ayala's Painting started in 2012 with one truck and a simple bet: do the prep other painters skip, put the warranty in writing, and the Front Range would keep calling. Fourteen years later the trucks paint apartment communities, city buildings, and schools. The bet hasn't changed.

Who You're Working With

Two Names, No Runaround

Ivan Ayala owns the company and still leads estimates and field quality himself. If Ivan walks your property, Ivan's standard is what gets delivered, and clients keep hiring us because that standard doesn't slip between the bid and the final coat.

Jonathan Martinez runs operations: scheduling, billing, and the paperwork that makes commercial clients' accounting teams happy. Weekly invoices that reconcile, COIs that show up before you ask twice, and a straight answer on timeline every time you call.

The crew behind them is the same set of names week to week. No rotating cast of subs, no stranger on your property who doesn't know the scope.

  • Residential: 10-year exterior and 5-year interior warranties
  • Commercial: 5-year workmanship warranty, in writing
  • Named crew and a direct line to the owner
